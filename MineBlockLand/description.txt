MineBlockLand - a top-down co-op survival island.

Every world is generated from a seed: flowering grassland, palm-lined beaches,
cactus deserts, rocky hills, farmland, a shallow sea you can wade through and a
deep sea you cannot pass. Chop trees, mine rock, craft tools (better gear works
faster, tires you less and finds rarer gems), farm melons, and loot the stone
dungeon east of the camp - including the clay jugs in the room corners, some of
which hold nothing at all.

The island is alive. Crabs and turtles scuttle along the shore, rabbits, foxes
and monkeys roam the meadows in packs, ostriches sprint across the dunes, and
if you are lucky a unicorn crosses your path. Hit one and it bolts. Hit a rhino
or an elephant and every last member of the herd comes for you - until you put
enough ground between you and them.

At night the dead walk, ghosts drift straight through your walls and witches
throw bolts out of the dark. Survive six nights - at nightfall of day 7 the
Guardian of the Isle rises and fills the sky with projectiles. Slay it for the
confetti, the scoreboard and the glory. The world is saved on the host, so you
can come back days later and continue where you left off.

Commands: /friendlyfire (host), /newworld (host), /seed, /stats, /openallmap
